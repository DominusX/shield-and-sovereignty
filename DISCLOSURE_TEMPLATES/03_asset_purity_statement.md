# 🛡️ Storefront Disclosure Template: Asset Purity & False Hunt Protection

### 📋 How to Use This Template
Copy the markdown framework below and feature it prominently on your Steam Store description, pin it to the top of your Steam Community Hub, or deploy it during your game's boot sequence. This template is built for independent developers whose games are 100% traditionally hand-crafted, but who find themselves targeted by hyper-vigilant, paranoid audience witch-hunts due to low-poly models, indie shortcuts, or compression artifacts. Replace all bracketed text `[like this]` with your specific project data.

The development of *[Game Title]* is anchored entirely in direct human craftsmanship, manual execution, and creative intentionality. * **Visual & Mesh Architecture:** Every 3D mesh, 2D sprite, user interface panel, background texture, and environmental texture utilizing standard independent development shortcuts (including [e.g., low-poly models, low-resolution pixel scaling, asset store packs, and unity/unreal engine stock materials]) has been vetted and implemented via standard human pipeline engineering. 
* **Audio & Narrative Data:** 100% of the music tracks, vocal arrays, dialogue scripts, and localized lore documentation are completely composed, written, and structured by human agency.
* **The Absolute Standard:** This project contains zero (0%) generative artificial intelligence elements across both frontend creative assets and backend coding logic. 

### II. Dismantling the "Backrooms Effect" (The False Accusation)
* **The Diagnostic Boundary:** Independent game production requires optimization shortcuts. Minor visual artifacts, compressed texture tiling, stylized blur filtres, low-resolution rendering, or mismatched stylistic store-bought assets are standard technical side effects of indie development. 
* **The Non-Negotiation Rule:** Mistaking these everyday technical constraints or low-fidelity aesthetic choices for "Generative AI" is a product of uninformed, hyper-vigilant online paranoia. We do not apologize for, nor will we re-engineer, our optimized indie pipelines to appease baseless internet conspiracy theories or mob-driven guesswork.

### III. Terms of Consumer Engagement
This studio operates strictly on absolute technical truth and mutual respect.
* We actively welcome constructive, verifiable bug reports regarding collision boundaries, mechanical errors, UI functionality, and optimization metrics.
* We do not engage with, tolerate, or answer to bad-faith speculative asset witch-hunts, emotional internet buzzwords, or mob-enforced digital policing.
* Reactionary review-bombing or speculative forum harassment targeting the legitimacy of our custom-made assets will be treated as an absolute filter. If you choose to chase phantoms over playing a high-performance, 100% hand-crafted indie experience, this software is simply not designed for you.
