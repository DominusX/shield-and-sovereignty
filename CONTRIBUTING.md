# 🗺️ Contributing to shield-and-sovereignty

Thank you for seeking to expand this decentralized defense network. This repository serves as an open-source construction kit providing proactive disclosure templates, community policy blueprints, and intellectual firewalls for independent creators.

To preserve the absolute structural integrity of these tools and ensure incoming contributions remain practical, technical, and high-performance, all pull requests and issues must strictly adhere to the operational protocols defined below.

---

## 🛑 The Core Directives

1. **Utility Over Ideology:** Submissions must be entirely modular, actionable, and copy-pasteable for an exhausted solo creator. Do not turn template pull requests into long personal essays, emotional rants, or open forum debates.
2. **Absolute Structural Confidence:** Never submit a template utilizing defensive, submissive, or apologetic rhetoric. Every blueprint added to this kit must project an unyielding, calm posture of absolute creative and engineering sovereignty.
3. **Factual Pipeline Targeting:** Structural additions must focus on clear boundaries between frontend human craftsmanship and backend algorithmic utility (such as separating automated compilation, code structures, or asset packaging from creative human intent).

---

## 🧱 Engine-Specific Template Requirements

We actively welcome template contributions tailored to specific development ecosystems (e.g., Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot, RPG Maker). When introducing a platform-specific template, you must satisfy the following parameters:

* **Identify the Ecosystem Constraints:** Clearly state the specific store-bought asset dependencies, stock engine materials, or shader scripts typical to that engine that frequently trigger false audience paranoia (The Backrooms Effect).
* **Define the Tech Architecture:** Provide clear, precise vocabulary explaining why these engine-specific shortcuts or background compilers operate strictly as non-generative utility optimization tools.

## 🪵 Submission Review Workflow

1. **Isolate the Pipeline:** Propose your additions by creating a clean, dedicated branch on your fork before submitting a Pull Request.
2. **The Formatting Gate:** Submissions that rely on shifting internet slang, lack a clear division between backend engineering and frontend art, or fail to follow the mandatory raw markdown layout block will be closed immediately without merge.
