# 🏛️ Community Policy Blueprint: Steam Hub Forum Moderation

### 📋 How to Use This Template
Create a new file named `steam_hub_rules.md` inside your `COMMUNITY_POLICIES/` directory and paste this content. Independent developers can copy the text below the line, customize the bracketed fields `[like this]`, and post it as a **Pinned Global Announcement** at the very top of their Steam Community Discussion Hub.

---

```markdown
## ⚖️ Steam Community Discussion Hub: Rules of Engagement & Moderation Protocol

Welcome to the official Steam Community Hub for *[Game Title]*. This forum is an open, functional workspace dedicated to game optimization, mechanical feedback, community guides, and bug reporting. 

To preserve the intellectual integrity of this space and ensure an environment built on absolute factual data and mutual respect, this studio enforces a strict, zero-tolerance policy against bad-faith digital policing and emotional mob behavior. Read these guidelines completely before posting.

### I. Acceptable Forum Discourse (Constructive Engagement)
We actively welcome and value player contributions that focus on the structural reality of the software build. Permitted topics include:
* Detailed, evidence-based reports regarding performance bugs, optimization issues, graphical glitches, or crashes.
* Constructive criticism concerning mechanical balance, difficulty spikes, gameplay loops, and design pacing.
* General community discussion, player theories, puzzle solutions, and strategy guides.

### II. Prohibited Behavior & Structural Violations (The Filtration Boundaries)
This hub operates under strict moderation. Threads or comments that engage in the following behavior will be locked immediately, and the offending accounts will face permanent forum bans without warning:

1. **Speculative Asset Witch-Hunts & AI Paranoia:** Starting or participating in bad-faith threads accusing the developer of using "Generative AI" based on personal emotion, low-resolution textures, indie asset store choices, or compression artifacts. This forum operates on documented technical data, not mob-driven guesswork.
2. **Weaponized Slang & Toxic Buzzwords:** The use of low-effort, reductive internet slogans (e.g., screaming "slop," "lazy," or "incel") to insult the game, its development pipeline, or other players. This language demonstrates total intellectual bankruptcy and has no place in a professional software hub.
3. **Coordinated Outrage & Thread Spawning:** Spamming duplicate threads, copy-pasting mass review-bombing manifestos, or attempting to organize digital pile-ons to harass the creators or derail constructive gameplay discussions.

### III. The Moderation Mandate
* **No Debate on Moderation:** Moderation actions (locking threads, deleting toxic spam, banning bad-faith actors) are final and are not open to public debate or retaliatory thread spawning. 
* **The Absolute Standard:** This studio stands immutably by our build and our team. We do not negotiate with, nor will we alter our creative or engineering pipelines to appease, fluid social media narratives or emotional internet mobs. 

If your objective is to explore a high-performance, custom-crafted indie experience and offer real, constructive feedback, we are proud to have you here. If your objective is to chase digital phantoms or farm engagement via coordinated outrage, this community hub is simply not open to your presence.
```
