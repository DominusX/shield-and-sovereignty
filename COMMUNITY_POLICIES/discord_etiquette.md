# 💬 Community Policy Blueprint: Discord Server Etiquette & Live Chat Guardrails

### 📋 How to Use This Template
Create a new file named `discord_etiquette.md` inside your `COMMUNITY_POLICIES/` directory and paste this content. Independent developers can copy the text below the line, customize the bracketed fields `[like this]`, and paste it into their Discord server's `#rules` or `#welcome` channel as a permanent, non-negotiable standard of engagement.

---

```markdown
## ⚖️ Discord Server Code of Conduct & Live Chat Protocol

Welcome to the official Discord server for *[Game Title]*. This server exists strictly as a functional, real-time sanctuary for community interaction, patch announcements, developer updates, and collaborative gameplay strategy. 

Because live chat is a high-velocity environment, maintaining clear boundaries is essential to prevent emotional distortion and preserve mutual respect. This server operates under a strict, zero-tolerance policy against bad-faith digital policing, coordinated outrage, and mob behavior. 

### I. The Pillars of Constructive Engagement
Every member of this server is expected to uphold a baseline standard of intellectual discipline and human etiquette:
* **Evidence-Based Technical Feedback:** Utilize designated channels [e.g., #bug-reports] to document performance errors, mechanical glitches, or balancing issues with raw data, clear descriptions, or screenshots.
* **Respect for Creative Autonomy:** Acknowledge that while gameplay critiques are valid, the ultimate creative direction, asset choices, and technical engineering pipelines of the software belong entirely to the studio.
* **Mutual Respect:** Treat fellow players and staff as individual human beings. Engage in discussions with clarity, logic, and respectful terminology.

### II. Prohibited Behaviors & Structural Boundaries (Immediate Filtration)
Engaging in any of the following behaviors will result in an immediate chat mute, message deletion, or permanent server ban at the discretion of the moderation team:

1. **The "Backrooms Effect" (AI Witch-Hunting):** Speculating, gossiping, or manufacturing paranoid accusations claiming the developer is using hidden "Generative AI" slop based on low-resolution textures, indie shortcuts, or compression artifacts. This server runs on documented facts, not conspiracy theories.
2. **Mob Radicalization & Ping-Spamming:** Attempting to organize digital pile-ons, rally chat members into defensive tribal camps, mass-pinging developers/moderators over minor personal grievances, or spamming hostile review-bombing manifestos.
3. **Weaponized Low-Effort Slang:** Using reductive, toxic internet buzzwords (e.g., screaming "slop," "lazy," or "incel") to hijack normal discussions or insult the game’s development workflow. 

### III. The Moderation Mandate
* **Absolute Authority:** Moderation actions (mutes, kicks, bans) are executed to protect the server's stability. They are final and will not be openly debated in general chat channels. 
* **The Non-Negotiation Principle:** This studio answers to verified craftsmanship and technical truth, not to fluid social media trends. We do not alter our development pipelines to appease real-time live chat pressure. 

If your objective is to connect with an authentic community, share strategy, and experience a custom-tailored indie game, you are welcome here. If your objective is to farm engagement via real-time toxicity, you will be swiftly filtered out of this space.
```
